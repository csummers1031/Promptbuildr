## PART 1 — Promptbuildr context (so the writer understands it fully)

**What it is:** Promptbuildr (**https://promptbuildr.ai**) is a free web app that turns a
plain-language description of what you want to do into a **ready-to-use, tool-tuned AI
prompt** — plus step-by-step setup instructions and recommended tools. It's for business
AND personal tasks, and for every experience level.

**How it works (the flow):**
1. You type what you're trying to do (e.g. "cold email to book a demo", "5-day Italy
   itinerary for 2", "a logo design brief").
2. You pick: your **role**, the **AI tool you use**, and the **output type**.
3. It generates a tailored prompt, **formatted for the specific tool** (XML-style structure
   for Claude, clean labeled sections for ChatGPT, code-ready for Cursor/GitHub Copilot,
   dense visual descriptors for image AI), PLUS a "how to use it" step list and the tools it
   recommends for the job.
4. You copy it, run it, or **remix** it. Good prompts also get their own shareable page and
   land in the live **Feed**.

**The dropdowns (real capabilities — reference these, don't invent others):**
- **AI tools:** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Perplexity,
  Midjourney/Image AI, Other/Any.
- **Roles:** Marketer, Founder/CEO, Sales/SDR, RevOps, Content/SEO, Customer Success,
  Recruiter/HR, Product Manager, Agency Owner, Student, Creator/Influencer, Personal Use.
- **Output types:** Email/Outreach, Content/Copy, Strategy/Plan, Analysis/Research,
  Code/Automation, Agent/Workflow, Data/Spreadsheet, Image/Creative, Writing/Personal,
  Learning/How-To.

**The Feed (https://promptbuildr.ai/prompts):** a live, growing library of prompts you can
browse by role, AI tool, or output type; upvote, copy, remix, and share. Each featured
prompt has its own page. (It grows as people use the site.)

**Key differentiators (the angles to lean on):**
- **Tool-tuned, not generic** — the same request is formatted differently for Claude vs
  ChatGPT vs an image AI, because they respond to different structures.
- **Instructions included** — you get *how to use* the prompt, not just the text.
- **Generated for YOUR task** — not a static list of 100 prompts you have to dig through.
- **For everyone** — business and personal, beginner to pro. No prompt-engineering skill
  required.
- **Free.** (First prompt is free; a quick free email signup keeps you generating.)

**Honesty guardrails (don't overclaim):** It's a prompt generator/assistant, not magic. It
gets you a strong first draft fast and tells you how to refine it. Don't promise perfect
output every time; the honest promise is "a much better starting point, in seconds."

**Linking rules — IMPORTANT:**
- Links to **promptbuildr.ai are an OWNED property** (cross-promotion). Use **normal
  dofollow links — do NOT add `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`.** The whole point is to pass
  SEO authority from HD to Promptbuildr.
- Any **affiliate tool** mentioned (the tools Promptbuildr recommends) still gets HD's usual
  affiliate link treatment per the normal skill rules (`rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`).
- Link to the **most relevant** Promptbuildr destination per post (see each angle below),
  not always the homepage — this spreads link equity to the feed/category pages too.
- 1–3 contextual Promptbuildr links per post, naturally placed. Follow HD house voice
  (including the no-em-dash rule) and normal meta-description/author setup.
